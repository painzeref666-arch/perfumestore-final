EXOUSIA & CO. CANDY RED LUXURY THEME

This source uses a saturated candy-red palette and warm ivory contrast for a luxury storefront.
Primary candy red: #E0002B
Deep red: #B90024
Main red surfaces: #8C001B
Product-card red: #A50024
Dark canvas: #690012
Light ivory canvas: #F3EEE8
Light card: #FFFCF7
Warm ivory text: #FFF9F4

The color update changes styling only. Product data, pricing, inventory, checkout,
discounts, visibility logic, and database schema were not changed.
